Android closed test

Install in three steps

Use the same Google Account for every step—the account signed in to Google Play on your Android device.

  1. Step 1 Sign in and join the tester group Open Idle Party Dungeon Testers in Google Groups and join with your Google Play account.
  2. Step 2 Request closed-test access Open the testing page with the same account and choose “Become a tester.”
  3. Step 3 Download from Google Play After access is confirmed, open the Play Store listing and install the game.
